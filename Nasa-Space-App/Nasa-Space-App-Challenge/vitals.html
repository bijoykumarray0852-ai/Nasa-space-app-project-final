            Mission Milestone

            Glossary

            AREDAdvanced Resistive Exercise Device — the on-orbit strength training hardware astronauts use to fight bone and muscle loss.
            SpO2Peripheral oxygen saturation — percentage of oxygen-saturated hemoglobin in the blood. Normal ~95–100%.
            mSvMillisievert — unit of ionizing radiation dose. NASA career limit for astronauts is roughly 600 mSv depending on age and sex.
            GCR / SPEGalactic Cosmic Rays / Solar Particle Events — the two main sources of space radiation. SPEs are sudden, high-intensity solar bursts.
            EVAExtra-Vehicular Activity — spacewalk. Increases radiation and physical strain.
            Orthostatic intoleranceDizziness or fainting on standing up — a common post-flight cardiovascular deconditioning symptom.
            BMDBone Mineral Density — used here as % of pre-mission baseline.
            HRPHuman Research Program — NASA division that sets astronaut health standards.
            OSDROpen Science Data Repository — NASA's public archive of spaceflight biology data.
            METMission Elapsed Time — time since mission start, in days:hours:minutes:seconds format.
